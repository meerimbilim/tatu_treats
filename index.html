ok but what is talkan?

talkan = whole barley, slow-roasted until it's toasty and nutty, then ground into flour. grandparents made it for generations because it works:

no sugar spike

barley's superpower is beta-glucan, a soluble fiber that turns into a gel as you digest. that gel slows how fast carbs reach your blood — an effect so well studied that eu food authorities officially recognize beta-glucan for reducing the blood-sugar rise after a meal. steady energy, no crash.

satisfying

that same gel slows your stomach's emptying — it's why barley ranks among the most satisfying grains in satiety research.

heart-friendly

the fda recognizes 3g of barley beta-glucan a day, as part of a balanced diet, for helping lower cholesterol — studies show ldl dropping around 5–8%.

happy gut

beta-glucan is also a prebiotic: your gut bacteria ferment it into short-chain fatty acids that keep digestion gentle and regular. whole barley brings iron, magnesium and selenium along for the ride.
